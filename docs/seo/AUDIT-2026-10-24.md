## Already good (verified)
- Indexable since go-live (`index, follow`), robots allows all crawlers incl. AI bots, sitemap with 48 URLs.
- HTTPS + HSTS preload, www and old vercel.app → 308 to the apex domain, self-referencing canonicals on all pages.
- Exactly one H1 per page, `lang="en"`, Open Graph + Twitter cards, all images have alt text, WebP images.
- Organization schema (founder, address, email, phone, Italy sales contact, sameAs Instagram/Facebook),
  Product + BreadcrumbList on product pages, Article + BreadcrumbList on guides, WebSite schema.
- Content depth 650–1,500 words per page; founder named; factory location; buyer guides.
- Lab speed on 4G: LCP 1.4–1.8 s on most pages (good), home 2.8 s (3D showroom, ~1.4 MB); CLS 0 everywhere.

Result: re-crawl of all 48 URLs — 0 title/description/H1/canonical/OG issues.

## Not changed (on purpose)
- No review / AggregateRating markup: Google ignores self-serving reviews, and there are no published reviews yet.
- FAQPage on the home page left as is (Google retired FAQ rich results; harmless, no SERP benefit expected).
